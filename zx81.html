Keys

The keys are the machine's own: a letter at the start of a line is a keyword (on a ZX81, P is PRINT, J LOAD, R RUN, S SAVE, A NEW, K LIST). Symbols are SHIFT with a key, and typing them on your keyboard does that for you.

FAST and SLOW

A ZX81 runs BASIC between the TV lines (SLOW). In FAST it runs flat out and the picture goes while it works, as it did on the TV. The ZX80 and TK80 only show a picture while they wait for a key.
